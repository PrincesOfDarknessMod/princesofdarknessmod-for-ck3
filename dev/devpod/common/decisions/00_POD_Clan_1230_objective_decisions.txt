#############################
# This is just for me to try and wrtie down my thoughts so their clear to me, feel free to disregard the following couple comments
# PoD decisions are, often a little empty, cheap, as a consequence of the amount of them, each individual one has less work put into it, especially the canon char decisions (looking at you vykos) they're also entierly all focused on conquest as a major objective, with little attention being brought to the other sides of CK3 that aren't map painting. This is in part to rectifiy that, so decisions are meant to be more realm and personal char focued as well as fel more complete
# What makes a decision feel empty? what makes a building feel empty? in general when it comes to ck3 what makes a piece of content feel empty? it's always more stuff but psycologically it can feel like an empty box. Personally i think it's the amount and particularity of the requirements, as well as the specialness of the reward that gives decisions that complete and interesting feel a major decision should feel like a long term quest with several smaller side quests and a unique reward.
# In order to make the requirements feel complete and neither cheap nor empty there needs to be several of them, i think anything less than 4 is already empty, but number is not the only factor, they also need to be outside of the normal gamepaly loop, you should not be able to complete an objective by accident and finally they also need to be unique to that decision, this is in order to ensrue every decision keeps its unique feel and doesn't start looking like cookie-cutter copes of each other 
# The reward also is important the power of ti is obvious as well as the need for a thematic link to the decision and obviously it needs to be unique, but more than that, for large scale decisions liek these ones it also should, if possible, make for a reactive world, living world, the stakes of the decisions are large and they need to make the world react to them for the player to properly feel the weight, shamed gangrel, usurpers exposed somewhat work but they aren't felt enough by the player. there needs to be something else
############################# Reactivity needs to be seen and felt by the player, world shaking objectives need world shaking rewards, dynasty modifiers help, but it should be more visible than that, maybe TODO changing the base animation of the character? or changing the AI to react to the player? that's probably too delicate for it to be in 40 decisions though or giving access to a special court room, or a trait given to everyone that is affected, modifiers aren't visible enough to feel reactive sometimes

			count = all

			text = ventrue_landed_house_objective_decision_custom_2
		this.house = {any_house_member = {
			count = 50
		} #50 members of the house might be too little, the idea is to ensure there are enough people that this isn't ticked instant and that staying harmoniuis is hard but not impossible
		} }
		house = { 
			has_house_unity_stage = vampire_harmonious
		}
		this.house = {any_house_member = { 
			count >= 10
			stewardship >= 15 } }
	} #maybe add a requirement that prevents the player from doing 1 kingdom holy war and giving a county to everyone in his house? but any i can think of would step on the thematic space of the others, maybe need to be in the ephorate? or have good ephor positions?
