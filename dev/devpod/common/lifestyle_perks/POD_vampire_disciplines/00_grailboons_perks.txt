# Not a normal lifestyle. Perks are "hidden" and are only assigned when you become a Grail Knight

# When adding new perks, remember to also add them in the other places where they're listed:
# - The effect descriptions in common\effect_localization\00_POD_discipline_perk_effects.txt
# - The effect injectors in common\scripted_effects\POD_injectors\POD_injectors_lifestyle_perks.txt
# - The trigger injectors in common\scripted_triggers\POD_injectors\POD_trigger_injectors_perks.txt
# - The boon restriction triggers in common\scripted_triggers\POD_injectors\POD_trigger_injectors_perks.txt
# Do NOT check for the perks directly, at least in most cases. Use the boon restriction triggers
# The injectors are categorized by type and tier/Chivalry, e.g. POD_perk_injector_full_vampire_tier1 (or POD_perk_injector_trigger_full_vampire_tier1 for the trigger)
# - Tier 1 refers to 25 Chivalry XP. Tier 2 = 50XP. Tier 3 = 75XP.
