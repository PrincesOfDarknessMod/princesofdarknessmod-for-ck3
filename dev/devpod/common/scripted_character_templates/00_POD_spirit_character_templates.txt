####These are the various templates used for Spirit Characters
####You should not be using this directly with the exception of the very last one
####Find the effects that spawn these in POD_spirit_effects under the Spawning section
####All of these are saved as scope:spawned_spirit after creation
####If you want to use these as duel opponents, make sure you add prowess to them after spawning
