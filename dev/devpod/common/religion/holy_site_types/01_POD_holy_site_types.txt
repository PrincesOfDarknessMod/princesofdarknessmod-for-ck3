# Most are chosen due to being common holy sites in the region, being decently separated geographically, and/or being a grand city
# https://forum.paradoxplaza.com/forum/threads/the-activate_holy_site-deactivate_holy_site-command-affect-all-faiths-sharing-the-same-holy-site-and-not-just-the-faith-that-got-the-effect.1615700/

# - domain_tax_same_faith_mult as you gain more money from mummies you create
