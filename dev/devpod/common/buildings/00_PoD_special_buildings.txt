######################################################
#
# How it is built (the offering-gate model):
#   * Tier 1 is placed by the "Found the Well of Sacrifice" decision via
#     add_building — it is never offered in the build UI (can_construct_potential
#     = always no), so founding the Well is a deliberate narrative act.
#   * Tiers 2-4 are built in the normal build UI, BUT each is gated on a county
#     "offering" modifier on Constantinople (mary_well_offering_tN). That modifier
#     is granted by the recurring "Sacrifice the City to the Well" decision, which
#     pours a slice of the city's development down the shaft. So: sacrifice to
#     unlock the next tier, then raise it. The development bled is the true cost.
#   * Owning the tier-4 (open) Well is itself the unlock for the `demons`
#     men-at-arms (see 00_POD_regiment_types_special.txt) — no flag needed.
#
# Gated to Synthesis-path Mary as holder; an enemy who conquers Constantinople
# holds a dormant Well (is_enabled fails), so the bonuses are hers alone.
######################################################

### Cartagena - Lost Silver Mine of Cartago Nova.

	# Silver mines located Carthago Nova (Cartagena), the ancient Carthaginian capital in Iberia. 

	# WARNING: This is more of a suggestion. It scratches myth a bit, so rejecting it is well-understood.

	# Reference - Sierra Minera de Cartagena (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sierra_Minera_de_Cartagena-La_Uni%C3%B3n)

	# The name "Baebelo" is a Roman toponym. The mine's name is suggested by "Pliny the Old" in his book of Natural History (https://www.attalus.org/pliny/hn33b.html#:~:text=they%20are%20named%20from%20the%20persons%20who,nearest%20the%20surface%20is%20called%20the%20'raw.').
		# He suggested used to give Hasdrubal some ''300 bullions of silver a day''.
		# The historical location of the mine is unclear, whether it's actually near Cartagena or Jaen.
	
	# Notes 
		# Very exploited mines in ancient times by Phoenicia, Carthage and Rome. Medieval era? Not so much. Surface veins became rugged or inaccessible (never mind the political instability). 
		# In modern times, technology and greater exploitation enabled to exploit the mines again like never before (until soon they died out).
		# So this remains as a ''mythical location'' that yields greater income (whilst paying more costs & suffering maluses) and remains accessible only to Cainites (under specific conditions like use of knights & disciplines).
			# May open to other splats if suggested.

	# Extra Rationale: Iberia needs at least one mine, I feel. 
		# And any would-be city there could use a special building. 
		# Nevermind the Carthaginian Brujah lack memorable buildings (besides Hamilcar Palaestra).

	# Final addendum: The descriptions & requirements already tell a narrative, without the need of an event chain.

#karde: I like the idea in general but the growing malus made this prohibitive for what is a good but quite restricted (need many advanced disciplines) mine
#So I simply toned down the malus
